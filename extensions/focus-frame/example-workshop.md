# Assembly bench

**Fictional sample.** This page illustrates how the frame can draw attention to a diagram; it does not
describe a shipped product.

::::section{title="See the three parts together" id="bench" recipe="demo" focus-frame="corners"}
![Three separate blocks on a workshop bench connected by one horizontal line](assets/bench.svg)

Four short corner marks identify the diagram while it enters view. The diagram and its alternative text
remain present without the marks, including in print.
::::

::::section{title="Read the sequence" id="sequence"}
Start at the left block, check the centre connection, then inspect the right block. The frame decorates the
picture; the sequence is written here for readers who do not see it.
::::
