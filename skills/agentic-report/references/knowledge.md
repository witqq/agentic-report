# Find guidance by the reader or viewer's task

Use this map to reach the relevant guidance at the decision where it matters. Page categories and examples are recommendations, not restrictions on combining vocabulary. A filmed scene adds timed development; a static or interactive page gives the reader control. Do not transfer a rule between those contexts without considering what it protects.

Each reference contains its topic's facts, recommendations, supporting sources, measurement conditions, limitations and operational contracts. A recipe demonstrates an arrangement; it does not decide the interpretation of new material. Browse examples for alternatives and select by their actual content, rather than copying one layout for every page or scene.
