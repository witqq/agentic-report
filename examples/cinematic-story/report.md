# After the last light

**Fictional sample.** This visual essay demonstrates local image storytelling. The place, expedition, and
observations are invented; replace them with owned media and verified reporting.

Four frames follow a survey team across a changing ridge. The images lead. Captions and observations keep
their meaning available without motion.
